🚀 Growth Plan: What We Can Focus On Next

1. Avoid "Chocolate Covered Broccoli"

Weave educational questions directly into core game actions (like shooting, racing, or dodging) rather than treating questions like separate pop-up speedbumps.

2. Standardize AI Documentation

Make linking an exported AI chat log or documentation Google Doc a mandatory step before submitting web design projects.

3. Conduct Early Playtesting

Test multiplayer mechanics and opponent AI timing with classmates early to catch turn-taking issues and bugged levels before final publishing.
